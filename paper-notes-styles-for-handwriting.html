Questions before you download

What paper styles can I write on?

There are 100 notebook styles. The collection includes minimal, linen, Japanese grid, academic, engineering, music, meeting, research, planner, travel, wellness, recipe, creative, and dark-paper systems. You can browse and preview all 100 styles before choosing one.

Is a style just a background?

No. Each style has its own cover, paper structure, color palette, dividers, stamps or stickers, toolbar accent, and widget appearance.

Can I set the page size like a paper notebook?

Yes. Use fixed pages in A4, A5, Letter, screen, or custom sizes, in portrait or landscape, or switch to an infinite canvas.

Which pens can I use on the paper?

29 distinct pens and brushes, including fountain pen, calligraphy, fude brush, technical pen, watercolor wash, charcoal, and mechanical pencil (2B/4B/6B), each with its own texture and stroke response. Pressure and tilt shape supported strokes, and palm rejection helps prevent stray marks.

Can I decorate pages the way I would a paper journal?

Yes. Add stickers and washi tape, and keep an auto-numbered table of contents. Ink layers have opacity, visibility, and locking, and there is a ruler and eight shape tools.

Can I keep a handout open beside the page I am writing on?

Yes. A reference view keeps a PDF or image beside the page, and you can import a PDF and mark it up with handwriting, highlights, underlines, strike-through, and text notes.

How many styles come with the download?

The download includes the core editor, 29 writing tools, PDF markup, page audio attachments, handwriting search, and study tools, with 1 active notebook of up to 5 pages and 2 of the 100 styles. An optional one-time upgrade adds unlimited notebooks and pages, all 100 styles, vector-ink PDF export, restorable page history, and encrypted backup.
