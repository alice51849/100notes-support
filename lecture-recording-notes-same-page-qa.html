Questions before you download

Can I record a lecture and keep the audio on the same page as my notes?

Yes. Audio recordings are attached to the page you choose, so the recording from class stays with the handwritten page you wrote it on instead of in a separate app.

Can I write on the lecture slides PDF with Apple Pencil?

Yes. Import a PDF and mark it up with handwriting, highlights, underlines, strike-through, and text notes. You can also rotate, crop, insert, extract, merge, and export pages.

Will my handwriting be searchable later?

Yes. On-device handwriting and PDF text recognition lets you search across titles, body text, handwriting, and attachments.

Can I study from the same notebook before an exam?

Yes. Linked flashcards, cloze cards, and FSRS spaced repetition are built in, so you review from the notes you already wrote.

Does it work offline, and do I need an account?

No account is needed. Editing, search, and study work offline, and notes stay on your device unless you choose to export or back them up. There are no ads, no third-party tracking or analytics, and no external AI.

What does the download include?

The download includes the core editor, 29 writing tools, PDF markup, page audio attachments, handwriting search, and study tools, with 1 active notebook of up to 5 pages and 2 of the 100 styles. An optional one-time upgrade adds unlimited notebooks and pages, all 100 styles, vector-ink PDF export, restorable page history, and encrypted backup.

What lives in one notebook

  • Handwriting: 29 pens and brushes, from fountain pen and mechanical pencil to fude brush and watercolor. Pressure and tilt shape supported strokes, and palm rejection helps prevent stray marks.
  • PDF markup: write directly on imported lecture PDFs, with a reference view that keeps a PDF or image beside the page.
  • Page audio: recordings attached to the page you choose.
  • Flashcards: linked flashcards, cloze cards, and FSRS spaced repetition.
  • 100 notebook styles: including academic, Japanese grid, engineering, and meeting layouts.
