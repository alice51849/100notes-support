iPad handwriting notes apps: subscription or one-time purchase?

The big handwriting note apps for iPad are free to download. The difference is what you pay for once you rely on them: a yearly plan, a one-time purchase, or a mix of both. Here is how two of the most popular ones charge on the US App Store, and when our one-time-purchase app 100 Notes Studio is, and is not, the better fit.

100 Notes Studio is our app. We describe the other apps using only what their public App Store pages say, and we list our own weak spots too. Checked on November 27, 2026 on the US App Store. Prices, offers and ratings change; check the current listing before you buy. App Store pages show up to 10 purchase items, so a list may not be complete.

First, what do you need?

  • Working on the same notes with other people. Goodnotes describes real-time collaboration and sending a link so others can work on the same notes.
  • Your notes on a Mac or in a web browser too. Notability describes access across iPad, iPhone, Mac and the web. Goodnotes describes iCloud sync across iPad, iPhone and Mac.
  • AI summaries or live transcripts. Both Goodnotes and Notability describe AI features. 100 Notes Studio uses no external AI.
  • Handwriting and PDF markup on your own device, offline, paid for once. That is where 100 Notes Studio fits.

Where 100 Notes Studio is a good fit

  • You pay once, or not at all. The free version includes the core editor, 29 writing tools, PDF markup, page audio attachments, handwriting search and study tools. The optional one-time upgrade adds unlimited notebooks and pages, all 100 styles, vector-ink PDF export, restorable page history and encrypted backup.
  • Writing that feels like pen on paper. 29 pens and brushes, from fountain pen and fude brush to watercolor wash and mechanical pencil (2B/4B/6B). Pressure and tilt shape supported strokes, and palm rejection helps prevent stray marks.
  • Study from your own notes. Mark up lecture PDFs, record audio on the page you are writing, and review with flashcards, cloze cards and FSRS spaced repetition. Handwriting is searchable on the device.
  • 100 notebook styles, from Japanese grid and academic to music, planner and dark paper. You can preview all 100 before choosing.
  • Private and offline. No account, ads, third-party tracking or analytics, and no external AI. Editing, search and study work offline.
  • On iPad and iPhone, and on Android through Google Play.

Where 100 Notes Studio is not the right choice

  • The free version is small. It gives you 1 active notebook of up to 5 pages and 2 of the 100 styles. That’s enough to see if you like writing in it, but not enough for a full term of classes. Unlimited notebooks and pages need the upgrade.
  • No collaboration, sync or web version is described. Our store listings don’t mention sharing a notebook for others to edit, syncing notes between devices, or opening your notes in a browser. If you need that, Goodnotes and Notability both describe it.
  • Your notes stay on the device. Our listing says notes stay on your device unless you export or back them up; it doesn’t describe syncing between devices.
  • No AI. If you want AI summaries or live transcripts of lectures, choose an app that lists them.
  • It’s new. 100 Notes Studio has no US App Store ratings yet, so you have less to go on than with the apps above.
  • Separate stores, separate purchases. Buying on the App Store doesn’t unlock the Android app, and the Google Play price may differ from the App Store price.

A quick way to check any notes app

  1. Before you move a semester of notes in, open the app’s App Store page and scroll to In-App Purchases. Words like “monthly” or “yearly” usually mean a subscription. Words like “one-time” or “lifetime” usually mean you pay once.
  2. Check how many notebooks or pages the free version allows, and how you can export your notes if you leave.
  3. Read a few of the newest 1–3 star reviews, not just the average rating.
  4. If you subscribe, note the renewal date. You can cancel in your Apple ID’s Subscriptions settings.
