Questions before you download

Does writing in it feel like pen and paper?

It is built around 29 distinct pens and brushes, including fountain pen, calligraphy, fude brush, technical pen, watercolor wash, charcoal, and mechanical pencil (2B/4B/6B), each with its own texture and stroke response. Pressure and tilt shape supported strokes, and palm rejection helps prevent stray marks.

What kinds of paper can I choose?

There are 100 notebook styles, each with its own cover, paper structure, color palette, dividers, stamps or stickers, and toolbar accent. They include minimal, linen, Japanese grid, academic, engineering, music, meeting, research, planner, travel, recipe, and dark-paper designs. You can browse and preview all 100 before choosing one.

Can I pick a page size like a real notebook?

Yes. Use fixed pages in A4, A5, Letter, screen, or custom sizes, in portrait or landscape, or switch to an infinite canvas.

Unlike paper, can I fix mistakes and find things later?

Yes. Pixel, whole-stroke, and precision erasers handle corrections, and the lasso moves, scales, copies, or deletes strokes. On-device handwriting and PDF text recognition lets you search across titles, body text, handwriting, and attachments.

Can I write on PDFs and keep a recording with my notes?

Yes. Import a PDF and mark it up with handwriting, highlights, underlines, strike-through, and text notes. Audio recordings are attached to the page you choose.

Does it work offline, and do I need an account?

No account is needed. Editing, search, and study work offline, and notes stay on your device unless you choose to export or back them up. There are no ads, no third-party tracking or analytics, and no external AI.

What does the download include?

The download includes the core editor, 29 writing tools, PDF markup, page audio attachments, handwriting search, and study tools, with 1 active notebook of up to 5 pages and 2 of the 100 styles. An optional one-time upgrade adds unlimited notebooks and pages, all 100 styles, vector-ink PDF export, restorable page history, and encrypted backup.
